# Instruction voiceovers

All current instruction files were recorded with the macOS Samantha voice, a neutral American female English voice, at 180 words per minute. Deployment files are mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. They are grouped by task under `resource/voiceover/`.

The recordings communicate the following scripts; on-screen instructions in the task files use the same task rules.

## Visual response

- `welcome.wav`: Welcome to the visual task. Keep your eyes open and look at the center of the screen. Press the Green key as quickly as possible only when the target image appears. Do not respond to the standard image or object distractors. The target and standard change at the start of each block. Press any white key to review the examples.
- `target.wav`: This is the target image. Press the Green key whenever the target image appears. Press any white key to see the example.
- `standard.wav`: This is the standard image. Do not press any response key when the standard image appears. Press any white key to see the example.
- `distractor.wav`: This is an object distractor. Do not press any response key when an object distractor appears. Press any white key to see the example.
- `combined.wav`: Review the target image, standard image, and object distractor. Press the Green key only for the target image. Do not respond to the standard or distractor. Press any white key to review the examples.
- `verify.wav`: Please tell the examiner which image requires a response. The examiner will then continue the task.
- `begin_block.wav`: Press the Green key only when you see the target image. Otherwise, do not respond. Respond as quickly and accurately as possible. Press the Green key to begin the block.
- `thank_you.wav`: Thank you. This task is complete.

## Auditory response

- `welcome.wav`: Welcome to the auditory response task. Press the Green key as quickly as possible only when the target sound occurs. Do not respond to the standard sound or distractor sounds. The target and standard change at the start of each block. You will close your eyes during each block. Press any white key to review the examples.
- `target.wav`: This is the target sound. Press the Green key whenever the target sound occurs. Press any white key to hear the example.
- `standard.wav`: This is the standard sound. Do not press any response key when the standard sound occurs. Press any white key to hear the example.
- `distractor.wav`: This is a distractor sound. Do not press any response key when a distractor sound occurs. Press any white key to hear the example.
- `combined.wav`: Review the target, standard, and distractor sounds. Press the Green key only for the target sound. Do not respond to the standard or distractor. Press any white key to hear the examples.
- `verify.wav`: Please tell the examiner which sound requires a response. The examiner will then continue the task.
- `begin_block.wav`: Press the Green key only when you hear the target sound. Otherwise, do not respond. Keep your eyes closed and respond as quickly and accurately as possible. Press the Green key to begin the block.
- `close_eyes.wav`: Please close your eyes.
- `thank_you.wav`: Thank you. This task is complete.

## Auditory no-response

- `welcome.wav`: Welcome to the passive listening task. Do not respond to any sound. Do not count, categorize, remember, or pay attention to the sounds. Close your eyes, relax, and let your mind wander while the sounds continue in the background. Press any white key to continue.
- `example_one.wav`, `example_two.wav`, `example_three.wav`: This is an example sound. Do not respond, and do not try to identify or remember it. Press any white key to hear the example.
- `combined.wav`: You will now hear the example sounds together. Do not respond. Do not count, categorize, remember, or pay attention to the sounds. Relax and let your mind wander. Press any white key to continue.
- `verify.wav`: During this task, do not respond to any sound and do not pay attention to the sounds. Close your eyes, relax, and let your mind wander. Please tell the examiner verbally what you should do.
- `begin_block.wav`: Do not respond to any sound. Do not count, categorize, remember, or pay attention to the sounds. Close your eyes, relax, and let your mind wander. Press the Green key to begin the block.
- `close_eyes.wav`: Please close your eyes.
- `thank_you.wav`: Thank you. This task is complete.
